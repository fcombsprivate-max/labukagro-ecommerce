# Labuk Agro Ltd — E-Commerce Web Storefront

A modern, high-converting e-commerce web application inspired directly by **[labukagro.com](https://labukagro.com/)**, upgrading Labuk Agro's digital presence into a retail & wholesale storefront.

---

## 🌾 Features & Architecture

### 1. Direct Inspiration from Labuk Agro
- **Authentic Brand Identity**: True to Labuk Agro Ltd's original color palette (forest green `#1b4332`, leaf green `#2d6a4f`, harvest gold `#d97706`, and clean organic background `#faf9f5`).
- **Real Visuals & Assets**: Uses official Labuk Agro logos and actual product photography for **Cold-Pressed Castor Oil**, **Virgin Neem Oil**, **Raw Forest Honey**, and **Wholesale Farm Produce**.
- **Signature Homepage Pillars**: Faithfully highlights the two flagship services featured prominently on `labukagro.com`:
  1. *Castor Oil Products & Sales*
  2. *Agricultural Consultation & Training*
- **6 Core Principles**: *Experienced*, *Eco-Conscious*, *Core Strength*, *Best Practices*, *Health First*, and *Global Standards*.

### 2. Modern E-Commerce Capabilities
- **Multi-Currency Pricing**: Real-time switching between **Nigerian Naira (₦ NGN)** and **US Dollars ($ USD)**.
- **Product Catalog & Filters**:
  - Filter by *All Products*, *Cold-Pressed Oils*, *Honey & Extracts*, *Natural Spices*, and *Bulk Farm Produce*.
  - Sort by *Featured*, *Price (Low/High)*, and *Customer Rating*.
  - Live instant keyword search.
- **Size / Volume Variant Selection**:
  - Castor & Neem Oils: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1 Liter, and 5L Gallons.
  - Honey: 500g Jar, 1kg Jar, and 5kg Bulk Tubs.
  - Spices: 200g, 500g, and 1kg Value Packs.
  - Bulk Commodities: 50kg Sacks, 100kg Sacks, and Metric Tons (20 Sacks).
- **Interactive Quick View Modal**: Full product specifications, extraction techniques, beauty/hair benefits, usage guide, and lab certifications.
- **Slide-Out Cart Drawer**:
  - Real-time cart total calculation.
  - Free delivery threshold progress bar (Free nationwide delivery on orders over ₦50,000 / $50).
  - Promo code discounts (e.g. `LABUK10` for 10% off, `FIRSTHARVEST` for 15% off).
- **Checkout & Order Receipt Generation**:
  - Address collection (Nigeria nationwide coverage + West Africa / International DHL export).
  - Delivery speed options (Standard Dispatch, Priority Express 24-48h, Hub Pickup at TIC Ado-Ekiti).
  - Payment gateway simulation (Paystack / Card / Corporate Bank Transfer).
  - Instant generated order invoice with printable receipt and tracking code.
- **B2B Bulk Commodities Section**: Form for metric ton grain orders and 200L oil drums with instant WhatsApp dispatch.
- **Agricultural Consultation Booking**: Form to book sessions with Labuk Agro agronomists for castor plantation setup and crop optimization.
- **Direct WhatsApp Float Button**: Integrated link directly to Labuk Agro's line (`+234 806 055 1016`).
